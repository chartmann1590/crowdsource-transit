Account Deletion Request

CrowdTransit

How to request deletion

Send an email from the address associated with your CrowdTransit account to:

Send Deletion Request Email

Or manually email crowdtransit.app@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address tied to your account.

What happens after you request deletion

  1. We will verify the request by confirming it came from your account's email address.
  2. We will process the deletion within 7 business days.
  3. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

Data that is deleted

  • Account credentials (email, display name, profile photo) — via Firebase Authentication
  • Ratings and reviews you submitted
  • Comments you posted
  • Check-ins and activity history
  • Photos you uploaded of transit stops
  • Crowdsourced stop submissions
  • Leaderboard entries and points

Data that is kept

  • Nothing personal. No personal data is retained after deletion.
  • If you submitted content anonymously (without an account), it cannot be linked to you and will remain visible but disassociated from any account.

Additional retention periods

  • Crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics) — retained for approximately 90 days, then automatically purged. These are not linked to your account.
  • Backup copies — Firebase may retain encrypted backup data for up to 30 days after deletion before purging.

Questions? Email crowdtransit.app@gmail.com.
